Installed

  1. Start DaVinci Resolve. In Preferences > System > Audio Plugins, Automixer is listed as VST and "Loaded successfully".
  2. On the Fairlight page, add Automixer (Effects Library > Audio FX > VST Effects) to the Effects slot of each mic track in the mixer, not onto the audio clips.
  3. Give all mics of one conversation the same Group. With Link on (default) they share their settings.

Order on the track: cleanup (noise reduction) → Automixer → compressor (mixer Order "FX DY EQ"). Do not put a compressor or Dialogue Leveler before the Automixer. Set the final loudness on the bus.

Updates: "Check for Updates" at the bottom of the plugin window. More in /Library/Application Support/Automixer/ReadMe.txt.
