Automixer for DaVinci Resolve

A gain-sharing automixer for interviews and podcasts with one lav per person. Put it on every mic track in the Fairlight mixer: the person talking stays open, the other mics are turned down, and people talking at the same time share the level.

This installs, for all users:

  • the Automixer VST3 plugin in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • the sync check tool, a read-me and an uninstaller in /Library/Application Support/Automixer

Quit DaVinci Resolve before installing.
